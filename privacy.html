How SpartanReferee uses your information

This notice is being confirmed with Spartan. The parts still to confirm say so, and this page will be updated when they are.

Who runs it

SpartanReferee is run for Spartan, who decides how your information is used (the data controller). Which Spartan company that is, is being confirmed. For anything about your information, contact the SpartanReferee administrators: the people who added you to SpartanReferee.

What we keep

Your name and email address, and your profile picture if your account has one, as your Microsoft or Google account gives them when you sign in. Nothing else from your Microsoft or Google account is read or kept, and SpartanReferee never posts anything on your behalf.

The region and country an administrator records, and what you do on the learning platform: the programmes you're enrolled in, the lessons you complete, your quick-check and refresher practice (with the weeks you learned and the badges you earned), your assessment answers and results, and the credentials you earn.

For the burpee cameras: which events you're assigned to, as head referee or referee, and the bookings you make. Every change an administrator makes is recorded in an audit log.

Why

To train and assess referees, to record who is qualified to referee Spartan events, and to run the camera booking for events. The legal basis for this is being confirmed with Spartan.

Who sees it

You see your own record. Administrators see everyone's; a regional coordinator sees people in their region; programme owners and assessors see what their programme needs. Your information isn't sold or shared with anyone else.

It is stored in Microsoft Azure: the database and files in the UK, and the camera servers in Italy during events.

How long

Narration audio and camera recordings are kept for 7 days. How long your learning record is kept after you stop refereeing is being confirmed with Spartan; it's kept while you're a referee so your qualifications can be checked.

Your rights

You can download everything the learning platform holds about you from My profile, Download my data. You can change your preferred name and language yourself; to correct anything else, ask an administrator.

To have your information erased, or to object, ask the SpartanReferee administrators. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.
