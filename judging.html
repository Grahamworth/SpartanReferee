Official judging

At a competition the burpee zone is judged with two apps: phones film it with Spartans Broadcaster, and a laptop runs the Burpee Judge Receiver. The Receiver records every camera, runs the AI judge on each one, reads each athlete's number from the head band in front of them, and gives referees live view, replay and incident marks, on the laptop and an iPad.
