Camera phone

A few steps to make this phone a camera for the judging laptop. You can tick each one off as you go.

  1. Install Tailscale

    It links this phone privately to the judging laptop over any Wi-Fi or 4G. Open it, sign in with the same account as the laptop (the laptop's Receiver app shows which), allow the VPN, and check it says Connected.

    Get Tailscale

  1. Install Larix Broadcaster

    The free camera app that sends the video (Android or iPhone). Open it once and allow the camera and microphone.

    Get Larix

  2. Scan this phone's camera code

    On the laptop, each camera has a Larix QR code. Scan it with this phone's camera (or Larix's own scanner) and open the link in Larix.

    Then tap Import. The connections are only added once you do. Larix then sends to the laptop and YouTube, with a backup recording on the phone.

    Android: if the camera won't open the link, use Google Lens, or copy the Larix link on the laptop and send it to this phone.

    Then switch it on: Larix → Settings → Connections, tick Camera … to laptop (and to YouTube if there is one). Untick any older connections.

  3. Go live

    Put the phone on its stand in landscape and press Larix's red button. The laptop shows this camera LIVE within a few seconds.

  1. Install HeadJudge Broadcaster

    A small Android app that only films and streams: no watermark, no sign-in. The phone will warn you because it isn't from the Play Store: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install. If Play Protect asks, tap Install anyway.

    Get HeadJudge Broadcaster

    This is an iPhone: use the Larix tab instead.

  2. Scan this phone's camera code

    Open HeadJudge Broadcaster and tap Scan station QR code. On the laptop, switch the camera's card to the Spartans app tab and scan its code. Optional: tick Also stream live to YouTube and paste that camera's key.

  3. Go live

    Put the phone on its stand in landscape, tap Open the camera, then GO LIVE. The laptop shows this camera LIVE within a few seconds.

Keep it running

  1. Leave Tailscale on, and turn off any other VPN.
  2. Keep the camera app open with the screen on. Plug the phone into a power bank.
  3. If Wi-Fi drops, the phone switches to 4G by itself and the camera app reconnects. The other camera keeps going.
