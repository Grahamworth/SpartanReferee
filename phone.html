SpartanReferee Full setup guide

Camera phone

A few steps to make this phone a camera for your SpartanReferee server. It works over any Wi-Fi or mobile signal.

Android

  1. Get SpartanReferee BroadcasterThe phone warns you, as it isn't from the Play Store: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.
  2. Tap Scan the camera's QR code. On the judging computer, the video review shows each waiting camera's codes on its Live tab: scan this camera's.
  3. Put the phone on its stand in landscape, tap Open the camera and GO LIVE. The camera shows as live within a few seconds.

iPhone

  1. Install Larix Broadcaster from the App Store and open it once, allowing the camera and microphone.
  2. On the judging computer, scan the camera's iPhone (Larix) code with this phone's camera. Open the link in Larix and tap Import.
  3. In Larix, go to Settings, then Connections, tick the camera, put the phone on its stand in landscape, and press the red button.

The codes change with each booking, so scan again each time.
