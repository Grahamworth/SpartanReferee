Camera phone

A few steps to make this phone a camera for your HeadJudge server. It works over any Wi-Fi or mobile signal.

Android

  1. Get HeadJudge Broadcaster
    The phone warns you, as it isn't from the Play Store: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.
  2. Open it and tap Scan station QR code. On the judging computer, open the video review's Cameras tab and scan this camera's code.
  3. Put the phone on its stand in landscape, tap Open the camera and GO LIVE. The camera shows as live within a few seconds.

iPhone

  1. Install Larix Broadcaster from the App Store and open it once, allowing the camera and microphone.
  2. On the judging computer, open the Cameras tab, then iPhone (Larix), and scan the code with this phone's camera. Open the link in Larix and tap Import.
  3. In Larix, go to Settings, then Connections, tick the camera, put the phone on its stand in landscape, and press the red button.
