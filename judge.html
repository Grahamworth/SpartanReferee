    Settings
    
  
    
  
    
    
    
      
      
    Spartan default set and saved.
    
      
    One tap for race day: burpees, several athletes at once, race numbers read from the head bands laid in front of them, lenient judging (strictness 20), forgiving body tracking, Full pose model, count called out, video recorded.
    
    
    
    
    Station
    
    
    
    Burpees is for race use. The others are judged against their own standards, which the "What counts?" button shows. 
    
    
    
    Every athlete in view is tracked and counted separately, from their first good rep; their number is read from their head band when it's seen. Officials and spectators walking through aren't counted. Keep athletes from overlapping where you can.
    
    
    
    Analyse first: the app works through every frame (a progress bar shows how far), then plays the video at normal speed with each rep shown as it happens. Real time: the video plays at normal speed and the app judges only the frames it keeps up with, exactly as with a live camera at a race, so it shows how this phone would really do. Play while analysing: the video is slowed until the phone keeps up.
    
    
    
    Pick a landscape option when the phone stands on its side, as you look at its screen. It works even with rotation lock on. If the picture comes out upside down, pick the other side.
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
      
    Not strictStandardVery strict
    
      
    
        What this changes
        
    One slider for every exercise. The middle is the standard judging for each exercise. Dragging left gives the athlete more benefit of the doubt; dragging right demands a clearer, fuller range. Every rule moves together:
    
        
      
          
    • Burpees: how close the chest must get to the ground, how high the jump, how straight the hips, how high the hands.
      • 
          
    • Air squats: how far below the knees the hips must go, and how straight the legs at the top.
      • 
          
    • Push-ups: how straight the arms at the start and top, how deep the bend, how straight the body.
      • 
          
    • Jumping jacks: how wide the feet go, and how fully they come back together.
      • 
          
    • Sit-ups: how flat at the bottom, and how upright at the top.
      • 
        
    
        
    Each attempt records the strictness it was judged at, and the exact limits used.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    When one part of the body hides another, Balanced and Forgiving keep a hidden point where it was last seen for a moment, or place it from the matching point on the other side. Guessed points show as hollow circles. The Heavy pose model also helps when parts are hidden, if the phone is fast enough.

    What counts as one rep
    
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
      
      1. 
      
        
        Chest to the ground
        Chest touches the floor at the bottom of every rep.
        
      
        2. 
      
      2. 
      
        
        Jump, both feet off
        Both feet leave the ground at the top.
        
      
        3. 
      
      3. 
      
        
        Full hip extension
        Stand up fully: hips straight, not bent forward.
        
      
        4. 
      
      4. 
      
        
        Hands over the ears
        The tops of the hands reach ear height in the jump. Just below is fine.
        
      
        5. 
    
      
    
      Miss any one and it's a no rep, shown with a red cross and the reason.
