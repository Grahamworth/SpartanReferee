Set up the cameras

Phones film the burpee zone and send their pictures to your HeadJudge server over any Wi-Fi or mobile signal. The server records them, runs an AI judge on each camera, and shows everything in a browser for the referees. There's nothing to install on the judging computer.

What you need

  • A HeadJudge account for your company. Register here: we check each one, then you can start a server.
  • A phone per camera on a tripod or stand, with a power bank and mobile data or Wi-Fi. Android uses HeadJudge Broadcaster; iPhones use Larix.
  • Any computer or iPad for the referees, with a browser.
  • A good upload. Each camera sends about 3.5 Mbit/s, and the venue's connection has to carry them all: try it there before the event.

1. Start your server

  1. Go to your server, say how many cameras and for how long, check the cost and press Start. Each server takes 2 cameras, and starting takes about 5 minutes, so allow for that.
  2. Press Open video review. With more than one server, each has its own review page and its own 2 cameras. Share nothing from the address bar: anyone else who needs it signs in to your company's account.

Your servers run for the time you booked, used or not, then save their recordings and shut down. Running over? Press Extend on the same page. The review page warns you 20 minutes before the end.

2. Each phone

In the video review, open the Cameras tab. Each camera has its own codes.

Android: HeadJudge Broadcaster

  1. Install HeadJudge Broadcaster. The phone warns you, as it isn't from the Play Store: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.
  2. Open it and tap Create account (or Sign in). You'll need your email and a password, and you'll be emailed a code to confirm it's you.
  3. Tap Scan station QR code and scan that camera's code.
  4. Optional: tick Also stream live to YouTube and paste that camera's stream key.
  5. Leave Quality on 720p unless the signal is weak, then tap Open the camera and GO LIVE.

iPhone: Larix

  1. Install Larix Broadcaster from the App Store and open it once, allowing the camera and microphone.
  2. In the Cameras tab, open iPhone (Larix) and scan that camera's code with the phone's camera. Open the link in Larix and tap Import.
  3. In Larix, go to Settings, then Connections, tick the camera, and press the red button.

3. Start judging

  1. On the Live tab, each phone shows within a few seconds of going live.
  2. Press Start AI judge on each camera, check its settings and press Save. The judge runs on the server, so it carries on when you close the page.
  3. Do a few test burpees in front of each camera and check they're counted.

Review and protests

Rewind any camera with the timeline, find an athlete by number, or watch how their reps were judged from All results. Recordings stay on the server for 14 days and in your cloud recordings for 30.

If something fails

  • A phone won't connect: check it has signal, and scan its code again. Codes change only if the server is replaced.
  • The picture stutters on the phone or drops: the signal is too weak for 720p. Pick a lower quality.
  • A camera shows but no picture in the browser: the venue's network may block video. Try the browser on a phone's mobile data to check.
  • The server didn't start: your page says why. Azure sometimes has no graphics-card servers free for a few minutes: press Start again.
  • The internet at the venue fails: nothing reaches the server until it's back. Each phone keeps filming its own backup only in Larix, so keep one running on an iPhone if you can.
