HeadJudge My console

Set up the cameras

Phones film the event and send their pictures to your HeadJudge server over any Wi-Fi or mobile signal. The server records them, runs an AI judge on each camera, and shows everything in a browser for the referees. There's nothing to install on the judging computer.

What you need

  • A HeadJudge account for your company. Register here: your first booking asks for a card.
  • A phone per camera on a tripod or stand, with a power bank and mobile data or Wi-Fi. Android uses HeadJudge Broadcaster; iPhones use Larix.
  • Any computer, tablet or phone for the referees, with a browser.
  • A good upload. Each camera sends about 3.5 Mbit/s, and the venue's connection has to carry them all: try it there before the event.

1. Book your cameras

  1. On your console, choose how many cameras and for how long, check the cost and press Book and start. Starting takes about 5 minutes, so allow for that.
  2. Press Open video review. Each server takes 2 cameras, so with 3 or more you get a review page per server, and the cameras are numbered across the booking (the second server's are Camera 3 and 4).

Your cameras run for the time you booked, then the recordings are saved and everything shuts down. Running over? Press Extend on the console. Finished early? Press Stop, and you pay only for the time used. The review page warns you 20 minutes before the end.

2. Connect each phone

In the video review, every camera waiting for its phone shows its QR codes on the Live tab (they're on the Cameras tab too). The codes change with each booking, so scan them again each time.

Android: HeadJudge Broadcaster

  1. Install HeadJudge Broadcaster. The phone warns you, as it isn't from the Play Store: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.
  2. Tap Scan the camera's QR code and scan that camera's code.
  3. Optional: tick Also stream live to YouTube and paste that camera's stream key.
  4. Leave Quality on 720p unless the signal is weak, then tap Open the camera and GO LIVE.

iPhone: Larix

  1. Install Larix Broadcaster from the App Store and open it once, allowing the camera and microphone.
  2. Scan that camera's iPhone (Larix) code with the phone's camera, open the link in Larix and tap Import.
  3. In Larix, go to Settings, then Connections, tick the camera, and press the red button.

3. Start judging

  1. On the Live tab, each phone's picture shows within a few seconds of going live.
  2. Press Start AI judge on a camera, give it a name and choose what it's judging (burpees, squats, push-ups and more). It starts from that camera's last setup. Advanced settings has everything else. Then press Start judging.
  3. Do a few test reps in front of each camera and check they're counted. The judge runs on the server, so it carries on when you close the page.

Review and protests

  • Results shows everyone judged on the live cameras, from a previous recording, or in a video you upload, which is analysed on the server.
  • Watch plays an athlete from 5 seconds before they appear, with their tracking and every rep and no rep, and lists everyone else in that video to jump to.
  • Find athlete number lists every moment a number was seen, on any camera or uploaded video.

Recordings and results are kept for 7 days. After your servers have shut down, Past results lets you search, watch and download them until then.

If something fails

  • A phone won't connect: check it has signal, and scan its code again from this booking's review page.
  • The picture stutters on the phone or drops: the signal is too weak for 720p. Pick a lower quality.
  • A camera shows but no picture in the browser: the venue's network may block video. Try the browser on a phone's mobile data to check.
  • The server didn't start: your console says why. Azure sometimes has no graphics-card servers free for a few minutes: try again.
  • The internet at the venue fails: nothing reaches the server until it's back. Only Larix keeps its own copy on the phone, so keep one running on an iPhone if you can.
