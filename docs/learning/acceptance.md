## Notes on the results

- **Demonstration only**: every credential is watermarked DEMONSTRATION, the policies are samples, and the content is built on the 2026 baseline documents, not approved 2027 rules (NFR-04, NFR-06).
- **Real records**: since 19 November 2026 referees on the site's list can use /learn, which creates a real record (name and email address) for each. Everyone else is invented. See [decisions.md](decisions.md), point 2.
- **AT-11 and avatars**: the swap is shown between the three providers built (Azure narration, device voice, captions only). An avatar presenter would be a fourth provider through the same adapter; none was trialled (no vendor account).
- **Not tested here**: a real Microsoft sign-in on the live site, screen readers, and real phones (the pages are checked at phone width in Chrome).
