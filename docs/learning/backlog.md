# Backlog

What's left after the prototype, in priority order. P1 is needed before any pilot with real referees; P2 before the platform is used for real credentials; P3 improvements.

## P1: before a pilot

1. **Rulings on the seven open disputes** (the Reviews page): burpee time penalties (two), where burpees are the penalty, skipping a penalty loop, obstacle categories, when a Spear Throw attempt starts, a spear touching the ground in flight. Owner: the Spartan officiating lead.
2. **2027 rule text**: load the 2027 rulebook as a new source document; draft each changed rule as a new version so the review items list what to update.
3. **Content for the other 21 OCR lessons**, from the 2026 programme and SOP, reviewed against the 2027 rulings. The 2026 presentation's speaker notes and the obstacle tutorial videos, with reuse rights confirmed.
4. **Privacy for real referees' records**: lawful basis, privacy notice, retention and who is the data controller (NFR-07, PER-08). Referees are already let in with their name and address.
5. **The pilot language**: confirm Spanish (or French), and have a native speaker review and approve the translation.
6. **Pass policy**: the officiating lead's pass mark, attempts, wait and validity for OCR 2027 (the current ones are samples).
7. **Accessibility check** of the player and assessments to WCAG 2.2 AA with screen readers (VoiceOver, TalkBack), beyond the automated phone-width check.
8. **The learning page in the site's 15 languages**: the rest of the site is translated; /learn's own labels are English only.

## P2: before real credentials

9. **Merging duplicates** with an audited merge (PER-05: flags exist, merging doesn't).
10. **Service accounts for the read API**, so event assignment can check credentials machine to machine (INT-01, INT-02).
11. **Credential verification page**: a public link or QR code on each certificate that says whether it's valid, without personal details (ASM-07).
12. **Expiry reminders and refresher assignments**: email or notification before a credential is due, and targeted refresher lessons when a linked rule changes (LRN-08).
13. **Assessor calibration**: two-assessor decisions and reference rulings for video judgements (ASM-06).
14. **Server-rendered archive copies** of handbooks and certificates.
15. **DEKA**: a programme owner, real material and their qualification policy.

## P3: improvements

21. Offline use of a downloaded lesson on a phone at the venue.
