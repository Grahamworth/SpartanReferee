# SpartanReferee: UI/UX review (phones first)

Sources: code in C:\Repo\SpartanReferee (read only) and the phone screenshots in scratchpad\mobile\final-390, head-390, learn-360 and learn-320.
Severity: **High** = blocks or seriously confuses a task. **Medium** = friction. **Low** = polish.
"Verified" means I saw it in a screenshot or traced it in the code. "Suggestion" means it's a judgement call, not a defect.

---

## What works

- On phones the header folds into a Menu button. The button has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, Escape closes the menu and returns focus, and each row is at least 48px tall (headjudge.css:284-309, api-base.js:79-93).
- Inputs are 16px on phones, so iOS doesn't zoom into a field when you tap it. Most buttons and links are at least 44px tall.
- The event page gives a head referee one clear next step: a full-width **Book the burpee cameras** button with a sentence explaining it (head-390/event-referee.png).
- The status dots always come with words ("Not booked yet", "On now"), so their meaning doesn't depend on colour.
- Reduced motion is respected for the pulsing dot, the toast, the front-page video and the tracked-demo figures.
- Arabic and Thai drop italics and letter spacing (headjudge.css:275). The phone menu mirrors for right-to-left.
- Destructive actions ask first (stop, cancel, remove, withdraw, publish).
- The lesson player's main controls are 52px tall. It has captions, a transcript and a clear "1 of 8" counter.

## Findings (most severe first)

### High

**H1. On phones, the /learn tab row hides most of its tabs, including the one you're on (verified).**
- *Where:* learn/learn.css:89-92 and learn/learn.js:323.
- *What's wrong:* At 390px the row shows Overview, Programmes, My profile, Cameras and a single "R" of Rules (head-390/learn-referee.png). An admin has up to 12 tabs. The scrollbar is hidden, and nothing fades at the edge to say there's more. When the active tab is further along (People, Reports, Rules), the row still opens scrolled to the start, so you can't see where you are (head-390/learn-people-admin-menu.png: People is open but its tab is off screen).
- *Why it matters:* Referees won't find Rules, which is the tab most useful at an obstacle. Admins lose their place.
- *Fix:*
  - After each `draw()`, scroll the active tab into view: `$('learnNav').querySelector('a.on')?.scrollIntoView({ inline: 'center', block: 'nearest' })`.
  - Add a right-edge fade, e.g. `mask-image: linear-gradient(to right, #000 85%, transparent)`, flipped for RTL.
  - Better still, show at most 4 or 5 tabs and put the rest (People, Duplicates, Audit, Media, Reports) under a "More" item or in the existing Menu.
  - Add `aria-current="page"` to the active link.

**H2. Referees are shown booking, payment and "company" wording that doesn't apply to them (verified).**
- *Where:* console.html:76-81 and :94-99; console.js:17, :121, :304.
- *What's wrong:*
  - An ordinary referee on console.html sees "Used this month 0 camera hours · £0.00" and "Costs are estimates of what the servers cost to run" (head-390/console-not-head.png).
  - A head referee sees a price quote (£2.72), "pay nothing", "Book up to 90 days ahead", and a pitch for a "free camera that uses this computer's own camera… No server, no cost" (head-390/console-referee.png).
  - `role` is always `'owner'`, so `asMember()`, which hides costs, never runs.
  - The header shows the company name "SPARTAN" instead of SpartanReferee, and the sign-in heading says "Sign in to your company".
- *Why it matters:* These are HeadJudge customer-product leftovers. A referee standing at the burpee station is left asking "am I paying for this?" and "is this the right site?"
- *Fix:* For SpartanReferee, hide `.facts`, `#costNote`, `.quote` and `#freeNote` from referees, and hide the price for head referees unless Spartan wants them to see it. Run the `asMember()` path for anyone with `unit.canManage === false`. Keep the SpartanReferee wordmark, and make it a link to home.html. Change "Sign in to your company" to "Sign in".

**H3. "Choose times" works in the phone's own time zone, but the page's text and its errors talk about the event's local time (verified).**
- *Where:* console.js:37-38, :71, :200-204.
- *What's wrong:* The Abu Dhabi booking shows Start "19/11/2026 03:00" and End "07:00" (head-390/console-referee.png). The note above says the cameras run from 07:00 to 18:00 local time, with "(03:00 to 14:00 your time)". If the head referee types 07:00, thinking local time, the error says "Choose times from 07:00 to 18:00 local time on one of the event's days", which is exactly what they typed.
- *Why it matters:* Booking the cameras is the head referee's key task. They often book from home, ahead of travelling, so the mismatch is likely and the error contradicts what they see.
- *Fix (pick one):*
  - Make the time fields event-local: convert the input using `e.tz`, and label the fields "Start (Abu Dhabi time)".
  - Or keep device time, but label the fields "Start (your time)" and add the event-local equivalent under each field as it changes. Word the error in the same time zone as the fields.
  - Simplest for referees: replace the datetime fields with a day picker (the event's days) plus start and end hour selects, in local time.

### Medium

**M1. The "Start now" option can be disabled but looks only unselected (verified).**
- *Where:* headjudge.css:65-70 and console.js:194.
- *What's wrong:* Outside the event's hours, `nowRadio.disabled = true`, but `.segments` has no disabled style (head-390/console-referee.png). A tap does nothing and nothing explains why.
- *Fix:* Add `.segments input:disabled + span { opacity: .45; cursor: not-allowed; }`. Add a note: "Start now is available from 07:00 to 18:00 local time on the event's days."

**M2. The tag under the event name touches the card below it (verified).**
- *Where:* event.html:18 (`.tags` has a top margin only).
- *What's wrong:* The "YOU: HEAD REFEREE" and "YOU: REFEREE" tags sit flush against the Burpee cameras card (head-390/event-referee.png, event-not-head.png). It looks broken.
- *Fix:* Add `margin-bottom: 18px` to `.tags`, or give `.evHead` a bottom margin.

**M3. No head referee assigned: the event page says "The head referee books them" anyway (verified).**
- *Where:* event.js:121.
- *What's wrong:* In event-not-head.png the only referee listed is "maria.lopez (you) REFEREE", so nobody can book. The page doesn't say so.
- *Fix:* If `!p.referees.some(r => r.role === 'head')`, show: "No head referee yet. Ask an admin to assign one so the cameras can be booked." When there is one, name them: "Maria Lopez (head referee) books them."

**M4. Event hours mix 12-hour and 24-hour clocks, and dates are formatted three different ways (verified).**
- *Where:* event.js:101, console.js:27/172, learn/core.js:31-32.
- *What's wrong:*
  - The Camera hours card says "07:00 to 18:00", then lists "07:00 AM to 06:00 PM local time" (2-digit hours with AM/PM).
  - The console says "Thu 19 Nov to Sun 22 Nov … (03:00 to 14:00 your time)" in 24-hour time.
  - Home and event pass the chosen language (`lang()`) to the date formatter. Console and /learn pass `[]`, the browser's locale, so after you pick French in the menu, console and /learn dates stay in the phone's language.
- *Fix:* One shared formatter in api-base.js that uses `SR_I18N.lang`, with `hour: '2-digit', minute: '2-digit', hourCycle: 'h23'`. A race schedule reads naturally in 24-hour time, and it matches the copy.

**M5. The way back home is inconsistent, and /learn has no Home link (verified).**
- *What's wrong:*
  - The logo goes to the public front page on home.html and customers.html (`href="./"`), to home.html on event.html and /learn, and is not a link at all on console.html (`<span id="company">`).
  - The /learn menu holds only the language picker, your email and Sign out (learn/index.html:36), so the only way back to the referee home is the logo.
- *Why it matters:* At an event, referees bounce between home, the event, the cameras and the rules. A tap on the logo should always mean "my home".
- *Fix:*
  - Signed in, every logo links to home.html. drawSignOut could rewrite `.mark[href="./"]` when signed in.
  - Add `<a href="../home.html">Home</a>` to the /learn `.topRight`.
  - Make the console wordmark an `<a>`.

**M6. The lesson controls sit about two screens down, and move from slide to slide (verified).**
- *Where:* learn/lesson.js:129-151.
- *What's wrong:* Before the slide come the header, the tab row, the prototype bar, View as, the lesson card with its Under review note, and the Language picker. Back / Play / Next start at about 1,400 CSS px on a 390px phone (head-390/learn-lesson.png). Each slide's text and full caption box are a different height, so Next jumps after every tap.
- *Fix:*
  - Make `.lpControls` sticky at the bottom on phones: `position: sticky; bottom: 0; background: var(--panel); padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.
  - Fold the Under review note into a one-line link, and move Language into the tools row.
  - Show the caption box only while narration is playing. Captions duplicate the slide when it's silent.

**M7. In the lesson player, the Space key is taken over, even on focused buttons (verified in code).**
- *Where:* learn/lesson.js:162-169.
- *What's wrong:* The keydown handler is global and skips only input, textarea and select. Space on a focused "Mark as complete", Captions or Next button calls `preventDefault()` and toggles narration instead of pressing the button. Arrow keys also aren't swapped for right-to-left (in Arabic, → should go back).
- *Fix:* Return early when `e.target.closest('button, a, summary, [contenteditable]')`. Only handle keys when focus is on the body or the player. In RTL, swap ArrowLeft and ArrowRight.

**M8. /learn view changes: screen readers read the whole page, focus stays put, the title never changes, and the scroll position is kept (verified).**
- *Where:* learn/index.html:55 (`<div id="view" aria-live="polite">`) and learn/learn.js:319-328.
- *What's wrong:*
  - Every navigation makes a screen reader read out the entire new view.
  - Focus stays on the old link, and `document.title` stays "Learning: SpartanReferee" on every view.
  - Nothing scrolls to the top. Tap a tab while scrolled down a long page and you land part-way down the new one.
- *Fix:* Remove `aria-live` from `#view`; keep it on `#lpStage` and the toast. After drawing:
  - set `document.title` to `${h1 text}: Learning`;
  - `window.scrollTo(0, 0)`;
  - move focus to the h1 (`tabindex="-1"`, `focus({ preventScroll: true })`).

**M9. Primary red text is below AA contrast on the dark panels (verified by calculation).**
- *What's wrong:* `#e2231a` text on `#0b0b0b` is about 4.2:1, and on the card colour `#161616` about 3.9:1. Body-size text needs 4.5:1. This covers links such as "See the programmes", "Captions", "Transcript", the crumbs, "Sign out", "← Home" and "Spartan's page for this event". White on the red buttons (about 4.7:1) is fine.
- *Why it matters:* These screens are read in daylight, outdoors.
- *Fix:* Make `--primary-text` lighter (for example `#ff4d43`, about 5.6:1 on `#161616`). Keep `--primary` for fills and borders.

**M10. Correct and incorrect answers, and completed lessons, are shown by colour only (verified).**
- *Where:*
  - learn/learn.css:246 with assess.js:68: the result marks options `.right` or `.wrong` with only a green or red border.
  - programmes.js:93 with learn.css:146-147: the lesson tick is a `<span>` with `aria-label` but no role, so the label is ignored, and "done" is just a green fill.
- *Fix:*
  - Add text or an icon to each marked option: "✓ Correct answer" and "✗ Your answer". Add visually hidden text for screen readers.
  - Give the tick `role="img"`, or use visually hidden text "Completed" / "Not started". Add a ✓ glyph inside it.

**M11. Confirmation dialogs stay in English in all 14 translations (verified).**
- *Where:* every `confirm()` in console.js:344, customers.js, learn/assess.js:149, programmes.js:36/162-171 and lesson.js:209.
- *What's wrong:* i18n.js translates DOM text only. Native `confirm()` strings bypass it. "Stop now? Any cameras still streaming will stop being recorded…" is a high-stakes message.
- *Fix:* Wrap the strings: `confirm(SR_I18N.t('Stop now? …'))`, using the `{n}` slots for the dynamic ones, and add them to scripts/i18n-extract.mjs. Longer term, use a small in-page `<dialog>`.

**M12. No loading state and poor offline errors (verified in code).**
- *What's wrong:*
  - home.html, event.html and /learn show an empty page under the header until the API answers (`#content` is hidden, `#view` is empty).
  - On a dropped mobile connection, `fetch` throws a `TypeError`, and its raw browser text ("Failed to fetch" or "Load failed") is shown, untranslated and with no retry button (api-base.js:25, home.js:131).
  - The console polls every 5 seconds and fails silently (console.js:255), so its status can be stale without any sign of it.
- *Why it matters:* Signal at events is patchy.
- *Fix:*
  - Show a "Loading…" line or a skeleton.
  - In `call()`, catch network errors and throw "No connection. Check your signal and try again", plus a **Try again** button.
  - On the console, show "Last updated 12:04 · reconnecting…" when polls fail.

**M13. Copy contradicts the new rule that each event's head referee books its cameras (verified).**
- *What's wrong:*
  - customers.html:45 says "Any referee can book and start them from their console."
  - customers.html:74 says referees "can book and use the servers".
  - index.html:62 ("Book your cameras… see what the servers cost") and :64 ("Your server shuts itself down") describe the old self-serve, pay-as-you-go product.
  - The meta description at index.html:8 says "under your own logo and colours".
- *Fix:* Rewrite these in the event-page wording: "Each event's head referee books its burpee cameras; every referee can watch them live and review the results." Drop the cost and branding lines from the public page.

**M14. Admin: assigning referees means picking from 181 events in a plain select (verified).**
- *Where:* customers.html:61 (head-390/admin.png: "2 assigned · 2026 Abu Dhabi Spartan Wo…" is cut off).
- *What's wrong:* On a phone that's a long native wheel with no search.
- *Fix:* Add a filter box above it, like the referee search (`#findReferee`). Or default the list to the next 30 days with a "Show all" toggle. Put the event name first and the "2 assigned" count after it.

**M15. The /learn overview doesn't say what to do next (verified).**
- *Where:* programmes.js:28 and :40-47; head-390/learn-referee.png.
- *What's wrong:* The top card is a big "MARIA.LOPEZ" with "SR-000012 · No region · Learner". The next step is a small inline red link, "See the programmes". In the catalogue, **Enrol** is a ghost button, while the admin-only **Create programme** is the one solid red button (learn-programmes.png).
- *Fix:*
  - Unenrolled: show a primary button, "Start the Spartan OCR Referee programme".
  - Enrolled: show "Continue: <next lesson>" (the API already returns `nextLesson`).
  - Make Enrol `.primary`.
  - Shrink the identity card to a single line.

**M16. Ordinary referees see a booking picker and a one-item tab bar on the console (verified).**
- *Where:* head-390/console-not-head.png.
- *What's wrong:* The page is headed "Book cameras for", with a select truncated to "2026 Lakes Entrance Spartan Ultra Trif", though this referee can't book. The fixed bottom bar holds a single "Booking" tab, which navigates nowhere.
- *Fix:*
  - For non-managers, label the select "Event", or show the event name as a heading.
  - Hide `#appTabs` when only one tab would show.
  - Add a "← Back to the event" link (event.html?id=…).
  - The "What the AI judge can track" demos take up about two screens below the main content; collapse them into a `<details>` on phones.

### Low

**L1. Too many button styles (verified).**
- *What's wrong:* At least nine different looks: `.primary`, `.ghost`, `.small`, `.btnLink` (red outline), `.bigBtn` / `.bigBtn.second`, `.practiceBtn`, `.lpBtn`, `.clipBtn`, and `.link`. On the credential page, "Download the PDF" (grey outline) sits directly above "The attempt it was earned with" (red outline) (learn-credential.png).
- *Fix:* Reduce to three, shared across referee pages and /learn: primary (red fill), secondary (outline), and text link. Put them in headjudge.css.

**L2. Small tap targets (verified).**
- *What's wrong:* Lesson dots are 30px (learn.css:202), rule chips 30px (:198), crumb links 32px (:125), `.toc` 40px, and the `.chip` and `.badge` tags are about 12px text.
- *Fix:* Raise the dots and rule chips to 44px tap areas (padding or `::before` hit areas), crumbs to 44px, and tag text to at least .8rem.

**L3. Disabled profile fields look editable (verified).**
- *Where:* learn-profile-referee.png and people.js:87.
- *What's wrong:* A referee sees an editable-looking "Status: active" select and a "Legal name" box holding "maria.lopez". The status options are raw lowercase values ("active", "suspended"). The profile's "Language" field also competes with the header's language picker, and it isn't clear which one wins.
- *Fix:*
  - Style `input:disabled, select:disabled { opacity: .6; border-style: dashed }`, or show read-only fields as plain text.
  - Label the status options properly.
  - Rename the profile field "Lesson language", with a note saying it's separate from the screen language.

**L4. Raw identifiers shown to people (verified).**
- *What's wrong:*
  - The credential history says "issue" (assess.js:131, `h.action.replace('credential.', '')`).
  - The catalogue says "1 modules" (programmes.js:54).
  - "Hello, maria.lopez" falls back to the email address (home.js:112).
  - The sources note ends "4.2.4.." (lesson.js:158).
- *Fix:*
  - Map actions to words ("Issued", "Suspended").
  - Use `plural()` for module counts.
  - When no name is set, greet with "Hello" alone, or prompt for a preferred name.

**L5. Some state is shown by colour only, and yellow means several things (verified).**
- *What's wrong:* The Captions toggle's on/off state is only red versus grey (learn.css:214). Yellow `#f2b33d` is used for "Demonstration content", "Preview", "Pending", "Due" and "Under review" alike.

**L6. Physical (left/right) CSS breaks right-to-left layouts (verified in code).**
- *Where:*
  - learn.css:2 `.learnNav` margin-right / margin-left;
  - learn.css:7 `.viewAs`;
  - learn.css:26 and :201 `text-align: left` (audit table, certificate list);
  - learn.css:179 the question legend `float: left`;
  - headjudge.css:96-97 the tracked-demo bullets;
  - headjudge.css:105 and :247.

- *Fix:* Use logical properties (`margin-inline-start`, `text-align: start`, `inset-inline-start`, `padding-inline-start`). Use an SVG chevron that flips in RTL, as home.html already does.

**L7. Sign-in on home.html doesn't explain who can sign in (verified).**
- *Where:* signed-out-home.png.
- *What's wrong:* The card shows only the Microsoft button. The console's sign-in card says "Referees are added by an admin: ask one to add the address you sign in with."

**L8. Smooth scrolling ignores reduced motion (verified).**
- *Where:* assess.js:153, cameras.js:76, lesson.js:198.
- *What's wrong:* These use `behavior: 'smooth'` regardless of the reduced-motion setting.
- *Fix:* Use `behavior: matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches ? 'auto' : 'smooth'`.

**L9. Polish (verified).**
- The admin wordmark reads "SPARTANREFEREEADMIN" because "admin" has no visible gap (customers.html:20). Add a margin or a separator.
- The admin page has no h1.
- "Spartan's page for this event" opens a new tab without saying so; add "(opens in a new tab)" or an icon.
- In the phone menu, Sign out sits directly under the email, above the language picker. Move it to the bottom, and visually separate it, so it isn't tapped by accident.
- The lesson's Back button has `aria-label="Previous slide"`, which doesn't contain its visible word "Back" (lesson.js:142). Use "Back" or "Previous".
- The results page error "Cloud recordings aren't set up on the server" is developer wording (results.png). It may only appear in the test environment.

## Suggestions (judgement calls, not defects)

- **Home on phones:** at an event the useful thing is "my event today", but the Learning and Cameras tiles come first and push the events down (home.html:36-45). When an event is on now, pin a full-width "On now: <event> → Watch / Book cameras" card above the tiles.
- **Prototype bar and View as:** together they take about 300 CSS px at the top of every /learn view for admins (learn-people-admin-menu.png). Make the prototype banner dismissible, or show it once per session. Move View as into the header menu.
- **Captions:** turn them off by default when narration isn't playing, and use the transcript for reading.
- **Programme order:** list the real programme (Spartan OCR Referee) before the "DEKA Judge (placeholder)".
- **Names for the cameras area:** the tile says "Cameras", the admin menu "My console", the page title "Book your cameras", and the event button "Book the burpee cameras". Pick one name, such as "Burpee cameras", and use it everywhere.
