How it works

  1. Register your company

    Add your logo and colours, and you can book straight away.

  2. Book your cameras

    Choose how many cameras and how many hours, and see the cost before you start. Your first booking asks for a card.

  3. Film on phones

    Android phones use the HeadJudge Broadcaster app, and iPhones use Larix. Scan your camera's code to connect.

  4. Judge and review

    Watch every camera live and review the recordings. Your server shuts itself down when your booked time ends, and you can extend it if you run over.

What it costs

You pay per camera, per hour your servers actually run, so stopping early costs less. That includes keeping your recordings for 7 days, and there's nothing to install on a computer.
